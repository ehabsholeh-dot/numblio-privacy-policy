# Privacy Policy for Numblio

**Last updated:** September 27, 2026

Thank you for choosing **Numblio** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to providing a safe, educational, and enjoyable environment for children learning mathematics. We strongly believe in protecting the privacy of our young users and their families.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application, **Numblio**, handles user privacy and data.

---

## 1. Compliance with Children's Online Privacy Protection Acts
**Numblio** is designed specifically for children. We fully comply with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for Children**.

- We **DO NOT** collect, store, or share any Personal Identifiable Information (PII) from children (such as full names, home addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, or precise geographical locations).
- We **DO NOT** require account registration or profile setup to use the app.

---

## 2. Information We Store Locally
To provide a rewarding learning experience, **Numblio** tracks educational progress locally on the user's device, including:
- In-game rewards, badges, streaks, and stars.
- Completed levels and practice scores.
- App settings (e.g., Sound & Voice, Daily Reminders).

**Important:** All learning data and progress metrics are stored **locally on your device**. This information is never transmitted to or stored on external servers, and it can be completely wiped at any time using the **"Reset Progress"** option within the app settings.

---

## 3. Local Notifications and Audio
- **Sound & Voice:** Audio feedback and voice guidance are processed entirely offline on the device to enhance learning.
- **Daily Reminders:** If enabled, daily study reminders are generated locally by the device's operating system. No personal schedule or tracking data is collected or sent online.

---

## 4. Advertising and Third-Party Services
- **No Ads:** Numblio is 100% ad-free. We do not integrate any third-party ad networks or tracking SDKs.
- **No Third-Party Analytics:** We do not track user behavior or usage statistics via external analytics tools.

---

## 5. Parent & Guardian Controls
Parents and guardians have complete control over the application on their child’s device:
- You can turn sound/voice guidance or daily reminders on or off at any time.
- You can clear all stored progress instantly using the **Reset Progress** option in Settings.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage parents and guardians to review this policy periodically.
